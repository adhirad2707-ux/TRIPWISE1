# TRIPWISE: A Smart Travel Planning and Budget Management System

## 1. Problem Statement

Planning a trip involves managing destinations, activities, timings, and expenses. Handling these details manually can lead to confusion, missed activities, time conflicts, and difficulty in tracking the overall budget.

TRIPWISE is a Python-based command-line system designed to simplify travel planning and expense management. It allows users to create a trip, plan day-wise activities, manage expenses, detect activity time conflicts, split group expenses, and view a complete trip summary.

## 2. Scope of the Project

The scope of TRIPWISE includes:

* Creating and managing basic trip details.
* Setting a total travel budget.
* Creating a day-wise travel itinerary.
* Adding activities with starting and ending times.
* Detecting overlapping activities in the itinerary.
* Managing expenses under different categories such as Transport, Stay, Food, Activities, and Miscellaneous.
* Calculating total expenses and comparing them with the entered budget.
* Splitting group expenses among travellers.
* Displaying a complete trip summary.

The project is limited to a command-line interface and does not include online booking, payment processing, external APIs, databases, or a graphical user interface.

## 3. Target Users

TRIPWISE is designed for:

* Individual travellers planning short trips.
* Groups of friends or students travelling together.
* Users who want to track and manage travel expenses.
* Beginner Python learners interested in a practical travel-planning application.

## 4. High-Level Features

### 4.1 Trip Setup

Allows users to enter and manage basic trip information such as destination, number of days, and total budget.

### 4.2 Itinerary Planning

Allows users to create a day-wise itinerary by adding places, activities, starting times, and ending times.

### 4.3 Budget Planning

Allows users to enter expenses under Transport, Stay, Food, Activities, and Miscellaneous categories and calculates total spending.

### 4.4 Time-Conflict Detection

Checks the itinerary for overlapping activities and identifies possible scheduling conflicts.

### 4.5 Group Expense Splitting

Calculates each traveller's fair share of expenses and identifies who needs to pay or receive money.

### 4.6 Trip Summary

Displays the overall trip details, itinerary, budget information, expenses, and group expense summary in one place.
